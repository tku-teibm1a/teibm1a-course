## The three rules that matter most

**Your commit history is part of your grade.** Two teams submitting identical content will not receive identical marks if one worked steadily and the other did not. Both members must commit under their own account.

**Never edit a previous week's folder.** If Week 5 revealed that your Week 3 work was wrong, say so in the Week 5 report and correct `model-spec.md`. The history is part of what is assessed.

**Being stuck is not penalised. Hiding it is.** Commit what you have, and write down what you tried and where your understanding ran out. That is worth marks; an empty folder is not.
